# Narration: Kickstarter video (for review)

Calm, natural, first person plural. No produce, every drop $5 or more and real (see SOURCES.md). It ends on the
Kickstarter ask, not an ad end card. About 95 words in 45 s.

| From | To | On screen | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0 | 4.0 | Home, products tracked | Prices at Costco keep changing after you pay. When one drops in time, the difference is yours to claim. |
| 4.0 | 12.4 | Scan the paper receipt | So scan your receipt with PriceBack… |
| 12.4 | 22.9 | Upload the PDF receipt | …or upload the PDF from your Costco account. From then on, it watches every item, every day. |
| 22.9 | 32.4 | Alert, then $23 to claim | When a price drops, you get an alert. In October it caught thirteen dollars on coffee and ten on a kettle. |
| 32.4 | 36.2 | $1,623 to claim | And sometimes it's huge: six hundred dollars off a Samsung TV for just one day, and a thousand off a Generac generator. |
| 36.2 | 41.6 | How to claim at Costco | Show your receipt at the warehouse, and claim the difference. |
| 41.6 | 45.0 | The PriceBack icon | PriceBack is launching across Canada. Back us on Kickstarter, and be part of it from day one. |
