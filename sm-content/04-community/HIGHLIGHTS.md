# Highlights — moved to `05-highlights/`

The covers and the tray this file used to document now live in
**[`../05-highlights/`](../05-highlights/)**, which owns the Highlight tray end
to end.

> **[`../05-highlights/HIGHLIGHTS.md`](../05-highlights/HIGHLIGHTS.md)** — the ten
> trays, the cover arithmetic, and how to build them on a phone.

## Why they moved

This pack is a **dated run**. Its own [`CALENDAR.md`](CALENDAR.md) ends with *"Do
not extend it"* — the shared-predicament register borrows its energy from a
moment, and an account still using it in month fourteen sounds like it is
enjoying the moment.

A Highlight tray is the opposite: it is **furniture**. It is edited, not retired.
Keeping the tray in a pack that expires meant the tray would expire with it.

Two things also got fixed on the way out, and both are worth knowing about
because neither was visible at the size they were reviewed at:

- **The eight covers were eight different sizes.** Their icons ran from 56 % to
  91 % of the declared icon box. The only cover gate was a floor on total ink and
  all eight cleared it, because a floor cannot see a spread. The set is redrawn,
  measured and normalised in `brand/icons.js`.
- **The Français cover is gone.** A French-only drawer duplicated the back half
  of every other tray while leaving all of them still opening in English. Every
  Highlight is now bilingual end to end instead.
