# The hook bank — solidarity, and what to do when it stops working

The fourteen hooks in this pack will be spent inside a month. This file is the
part worth keeping: **why these hooks work, and forty more written to the same
rules.**

Read `../evergreen/HOOK-BANK.md` first if you have not. It covers the six
general mechanisms. This file covers the one this pack is built on, which the
evergreen pack deliberately does not use.

---

## The mechanism: shared predicament

Every hook in this pack is a variation on one move.

> **Name a thing the reader is already angry about, admit you cannot fix it,
> then point at the part that several people together can.**

Three beats, and all three are load-bearing:

1. **The shared predicament.** Not "you are overpaying" — *everything costs
   more*. The first is an accusation and the reader defends themselves against
   it. The second is a fact they were already carrying, and naming it accurately
   buys you the next sentence.
2. **The admission.** *We cannot fix the prices.* This is the beat that gets cut
   first and it is the one that makes the rest credible. Every other account in
   this category is promising to beat inflation. An account that says out loud
   what it cannot do is the only one whose claims about what it *can* do are
   worth reading.
3. **The turn.** *We can share them.* Small, concrete, obviously true, and it
   requires other people — which is the ask, arriving as a consequence rather
   than as a request.

The failure mode is skipping beat 2. "Beat inflation with PriceBack" is beats 1
and 3 welded together, it is the same sentence as every scam in the category,
and it is also a claim the Competition Act would make us defend.

### Why this works now and will not work forever

A shared-predicament hook borrows its energy from the predicament. It is the
right register for a launch into a cost-of-living moment and the wrong one for a
brand a year in — an account still saying *these are hard times* in month
fourteen sounds like it is enjoying them. Retire this register once the follow
count stops moving on it, and fall back to the evergreen pack's mechanics, which
are built to run indefinitely for exactly that reason.

## The rules every hook here obeys

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Two or three lines, under about 18 characters each** | The hook is auto-fitted to the column. A long line shrinks the whole block, and a hook set at sub-line size is not a hook. |
| **No number** | Not a dollar figure, not a percentage, not a claim window. Compliance requires the first two (`claims.js`); the third is banned because each retailer sets its own and one printed number makes the frame wrong the day store three lands. |
| **Never "get", always "ask" / "claim"** | We do not control the retailer's decision, so no hook may imply we do. |
| **The verb belongs to the reader, or to "us" — never to the app** | *We can share them*, not *PriceBack shares them*. The instant the app is the subject, the frame is an ad. |
| **It has to survive being FALSE for one reader** | Someone will read it having never had a price drop. A hook that only works if the reader has been burned excludes everyone you are trying to recruit. |
| **French is written, not translated** | *Personne ne bat l'inflation tout seul* is not a translation of the English, it is the same hook written again. A translated hook is a third longer and lands a beat late. |

## How to test a hook before rendering it

Four questions. A hook that fails any one of them is not ready.

1. **Read only the first line.** Does it make you want the second? The feed crop
   and the grid thumbnail often show little more than that.
2. **Cover the logo.** Could a bank, an insurer or a coupon app have written it?
   If yes, it is category copy, not this account's.
3. **Say it to somebody who has never had a price adjusted.** If it only lands
   for people who already know the mechanic, it recruits nobody.
4. **Run `claims.js` against it in your head.** A figure, a percentage, a number
   of days, "guarantee", "you'll get" — the gate will stop you, but finding out
   at render time means you already wrote the French for it too.

## Where these are enforced

Nothing in this file is advisory once it is in `scenes/strings.json`. Copy is
gated by `scenes/claims.js` (shared by the art gate and the caption gate) and by
`scenes/verify.js` gates 6 and 7. If a hook you like trips a gate, **soften the
hook.** The one time you loosen a gate to let a harmless case through is the
time the next case is not harmless.
